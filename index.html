Required labels

Every result must show

  • Election and ballot type
  • Province / municipality / district
  • Votes and valid-vote percentage
  • Results received / expected
  • Last updated timestamp
  • Preliminary or final status
  • Source and audit trail

Why official results are separate

South Africa's Electoral Commission manages elections and declares official results. VOTE SA can republish authorised public data with clear attribution, but this prototype cannot register voters, issue ballots or count legally binding votes.

Transparency centre

Trust comes from evidence

  • Publish the data source and retrieval timestamp.
  • Keep opinion polls separate from election results.
  • Show corrections and data version history.
  • Provide downloadable public result records.
  • Never expose a person's secret ballot choice.
  • Log administrative changes for independent review.
Privacy by design

No fingerprint collection

The fingerprint is a brand mark only. This app should not collect biometric fingerprint data. Device biometrics, if ever used for local app unlock, must never identify a vote or link a person to a ballot.

Offline behaviour

Cached pages and the party preview can be opened offline. Any offline poll response is clearly labelled as local-only and is not silently merged into a national count. On reconnection, a production app must synchronise only with its authenticated backend and preserve a verifiable audit trail.
