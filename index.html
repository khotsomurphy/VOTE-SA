Welcome to VOTE SA

To add this app to your phone, open the browser menu and choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen” when available.

Public opinion poll demo

This sample interaction is stored only on this device. It is not an official vote and is not included in a national tally.

Results and transparency

No shared live results are available in this prototype. Public results must be clearly separated from official IEC election results, with source, timestamp, status and correction history.

VOTE SA is an independent prototype, not an IEC service. It does not register voters, issue ballots, verify voter identity or count official election votes.
